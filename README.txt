Maa Welfare Organization - website with team area

HOW TO OPEN THE TEAM AREA (admin login, records, reports)
1. Open the site address and add #team at the end, for example:
   https://your-site.pages.dev/#team
2. First time only: enter your name, a username and a password (8+ characters). You become the Admin.
3. Next times: sign in with that username and password.

IMPORTANT
- Records are saved in the web browser you use, on that computer or phone only.
- Use the same browser and computer each time.
- Settings > Backup > "Download full copy" saves everything, including logins. Do this often.
- To use another computer: open the site there, go to #team, create a temporary login, then Backup > "Restore a full copy".
- The public pages show the same for everyone. Donations and requests from visitors arrive as messages on WhatsApp or email, and the team records them.
